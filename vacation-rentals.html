The Property & The Forest

A reforested, family-run property with a large garden, 1 km of trails and a small river — perfect for yoga, birding and quiet getaways.

We planted native trees and created trails that let you gently explore the forest. Guests frequently spot manakins, trogons, toucans and other colorful birds. The gardens are spacious — perfect for outdoor breakfasts, hammock time or a small ceremony.

  • Private trails & easy walking loop
  • River access for relaxation and birding
  • Garden spaces for yoga & small groups
